# Release Notes - v1.4.0

**What's New:**

🦥 **Meet the Sloth!**
Our slowest friend yet has joined the party! You can now dress up the Sloth in all your favorite outfits.

💎 **New Bling & Accessories:**
- **Gold Chain** & **Diamond Studs** for that extra sparkle.
- **Spiked Collar** for a tougher look.
- **Blue Bandana** for classic style.

✨ **Polishes & Improvements:**
- **Better Scrolling:** Drawers now have a handy "more" indicator so you never miss an item.
- **Smoother visuals:** Accessories now fade neatly at the drawer edges.
- **Layout Tweaks:** The "About" button has moved to the bottom-left for easier access.
- **Improved Trash:** Dragging items to the trash is smoother than ever.

Enjoy the update!
